oneqr

One QR code. Every social profile. Zero cloud.

1The problem

A person active on Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, and GitHub needs five QR codes, or a link tree that demands an account, a subscription, and their data living on someone else's cloud. oneqr collapses all of it: one QR → one tiny HTML page → every handle, straight to the visitor.

3Two honest ways to deliver the page

Mode A — zero cloud (default): the QR encodes the entire viewer page as a data:text/html;base64,… URL. Scan → the phone's browser opens the page directly. Nothing is hosted. No recurring anything.

Mode B — static page, no account: one viewer.html you host anywhere free (GitHub Pages, your site). Your links ride in the URL hash: viewer.html#ig=you&x=you. The QR points there. No database, no cloud account.

The builder app generates both, plus a download button for the self-contained viewer file.
